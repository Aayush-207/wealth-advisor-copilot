# Source note Income Tax Act transition FAQs

PUBLIC SOURCE NOTE. Author-created summary, not an original document or bank-approved guidance. Verify the original and amendments before use.

## Verified source facts

The Income Tax Department FAQ explains that the 2025 Act uses the tax-year concept for income from financial year 2026–27 onwards. Earlier tax years and related proceedings continue under transitional provisions of the earlier framework. Therefore, the date of a question alone is insufficient to select the tax framework.
