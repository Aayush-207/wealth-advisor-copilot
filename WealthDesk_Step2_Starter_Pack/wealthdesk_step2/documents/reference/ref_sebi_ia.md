# Source note SEBI investment adviser AI guidance

PUBLIC SOURCE NOTE. Author-created summary, not an original document or bank-approved guidance. Verify the original and amendments before use.

## Verified snapshot facts

The SEBI investment-adviser master circular dated 6 February 2026 discusses responsibility for client-data security and advice when advisers use AI, disclosure of AI use, and records of advisory interactions. Its records provisions include a five-year period and longer preservation in specified dispute or regulatory circumstances.

## Scope of this note

Refer to printed pages 20–21. These provisions concern investment advisers; applicability to a retail bank depends on the entity and activity. This note is not a universal retention rule or an assessment that the application complies with law. Compliance must check applicability and subsequent changes.
