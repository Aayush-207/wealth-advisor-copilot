# Evergreen Growth Fund terms

FICTIONAL DEMO ONLY. These rules and products are invented for software testing, not bank policy or financial advice.

## Product identity

This fictional product has ID DEMO-EGF-G. It is a market-linked growth product with potential loss of principal and no guaranteed return.

## Charges and lock-in

The illustrative annual ongoing charge is 1.20%. Each acquisition has a fictional 180-calendar-day lock-in. A redemption request before the lock-in ends cannot be processed. After the lock-in, the exit charge is zero. No emergency exception is authorised in this fixture. These are invented product terms, not ELSS legislation.

## Missing information

Minimum investment, settlement time, historical performance, and personal tax outcomes are not supplied. Do not infer them from the product name or from another product.
