## Answer evidence

- Capital gains: departmental guide, p. 2 (holding periods), p. 20 (annual long-term gains threshold), p. 21 (rate and STT summary). The figures are scoped to the cited 1961 Act framework, not represented as a verified 2025 Act answer.
- Fees: investor charter, p. 3 (fee disclosure in the adviser–client agreement). This does not establish any fund-specific rate.
- Offshore trusts: AML circular, p. 11 (trust beneficial ownership); KYC circular, p. 13 (trust documents). This does not decide foreign-exchange, foreign trust law or a client’s tax position.

The PDF files are unmodified originals. `frontend/public/documents/provenance.json` includes local paths, source URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 fingerprints. `scripts/verify_documents.py` verifies originals and citation text anchors offline.
