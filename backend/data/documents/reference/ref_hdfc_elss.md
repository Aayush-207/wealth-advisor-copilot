# Source note HDFC ELSS Tax Saver KIM

PUBLIC SOURCE NOTE. Author-created summary, not an original document or bank-approved guidance. Verify the original and amendments before use.

## Verified snapshot facts

The KIM dated 21 November 2025 identifies HDFC ELSS Tax Saver as an equity-linked savings scheme with a three-year statutory lock-in. It describes a predominantly equity-based objective, with no assurance of achieving that objective. It lists Regular and Direct plans with Growth and IDCW options. Its expenses section directs readers to the fund website for current expenses rather than treating historical expenses as current.
