# Demo bank investment knowledge policy

FICTIONAL DEMO ONLY. These rules and products are invented for software testing, not bank policy or financial advice.

## Scope

The demo assistant explains documented product facts, internal research rules, and general tax context. It cannot select an investment for a named client, authorise an exception, place an order, or determine personal tax liability. Personalised requests must use the qualified advisory process.

## Required context

Before stating a product-specific condition, identify the exact product ID, class, jurisdiction, and relevant date. Ask for decisive missing information instead of silently assuming it. Do not collect account numbers, PAN, contact details, or portfolios in the demo.

## Evidence and authority

Use the controlling product terms for charges and redemption conditions. Brochure marketing text cannot waive a controlling restriction. Use applicable dates, not upload order, to select a version. Equal-authority conflicting statements require escalation; do not average or choose whichever was retrieved first.

## Source review

Every material claim must identify its document version and section. The relationship manager should inspect the cited passage and its exceptions. Supported evidence is not proof of client suitability. Copying an answer must preserve citations, scope, dates, and the demo notice.
