# Demo source approval and versioning policy

FICTIONAL DEMO ONLY. These rules and products are invented for software testing, not bank policy or financial advice.

## Independent review

Received material is Draft or In review. A topic owner verifies extraction, numbers, tables, applicability, and source rights; an independent approver authorises publication. Public availability does not equal approval. The application must never invent an owner or approval timestamp.

## Effective dates

Approval date, publication date, effective-from date, and review-due date are distinct. Future-effective guidance is scheduled and cannot support a current answer. Effective-to is an exclusive boundary in this package: a version ending 2026-07-01 is not eligible on that date.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal makes the source ineligible immediately at the registry. Recheck before displaying or copying an answer. Preserve restricted historical evidence for audit, but do not reuse it as current guidance. Saved answers based on changed sources require revalidation.
