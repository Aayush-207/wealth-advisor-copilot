# Demo tax question context and escalation guide

FICTIONAL DEMO ONLY. These rules and products are invented for software testing, not bank policy or financial advice.

## Information needed

For a fund-sale question, ask for jurisdiction, tax-residency category, exact product and class, acquisition and disposal dates, and relevant income or tax period if these determine applicability. Do not infer residency from nationality. Avoid asking for account numbers or tax identifiers.

## Evidence limits

This fixture contains no tax rates, deduction amounts, tax liability formulas, or personal tax advice. A tax-saver name does not prove entitlement to a benefit. Withhold numerical conclusions when no reviewed applicable tax source is present.

## Escalation

Explain which context is missing. Offer a case to the demo tax specialist with the question, non-identifying context, answer ID, and source sections. Submit only after the employee explicitly confirms. A case resolution does not automatically publish new guidance.
