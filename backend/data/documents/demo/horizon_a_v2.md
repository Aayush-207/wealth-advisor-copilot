# Horizon Income Fund Class A terms version 2

FICTIONAL DEMO ONLY. These rules and products are invented for software testing, not bank policy or financial advice.

## Product identity

This fictional product has ID DEMO-HIF-A. It is market-linked and carries potential principal loss. Version 2 applies only to acquisitions from 1 July 2026 onwards. Acquisitions before that date remain governed by version 1; ask for acquisition date when a holding spans versions.

## Redemption charges and exception

For an acquisition governed by version 2, an illustrative exit charge of 0.50% applies to the redeemed amount if the request occurs within 30 calendar days after acquisition. The charge is zero after that window. Within the 30-day window, a verified duplicate-allotment correction is exempt only after written approval from Demo Product Operations. A relationship manager cannot grant the exemption.

## Other attributes

The illustrative annual ongoing charge is 0.80% and is separate from the exit charge. There is no fixed lock-in. Redemption processing requires complete instructions and operational review. This document does not guarantee settlement timing or tax treatment.
