# Horizon Income Fund Class A terms version 1

FICTIONAL DEMO ONLY. These rules and products are invented for software testing, not bank policy or financial advice.

## Product identity

This fictional product has ID DEMO-HIF-A. It is a market-linked income fund for illustration only. No principal or return is guaranteed. These version 1 terms apply from 1 January 2026 through 30 June 2026 inclusive; version 2 applies to new acquisitions from 1 July 2026.

## Redemption charges

For units acquired under version 1, an illustrative exit charge of 1.00% applies to the redeemed amount when redemption is requested within 90 calendar days after acquisition. The charge is zero after that window. Each acquisition lot has its own date; the redemption request date alone does not select the terms.

## Other attributes

The illustrative annual ongoing charge is 0.80%; it is separate from the exit charge. There is no fixed lock-in, but processing is subject to complete instructions and operational review. No minimum investment or settlement guarantee is provided in this fixture.
